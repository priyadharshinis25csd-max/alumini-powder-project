Production Excellence

Precision manufacturing from ingot to powder.

Every batch undergoes rigorous thermal melting, controlled gas atomisation, and vibrating mesh classification.

01

Virgin Ingot Melting

Selected commercial-grade aluminium ingots melted in temperature-monitored furnaces to ensure purity and minimal inclusion.

02

Gas Atomisation

Molten aluminium broken down into spherical and nodular micro-droplets using high-velocity inert air atomisation nozzles.

03

Sieve Classification

Multi-deck vibrating screens separate fractions into tight mesh brackets from +25 down to sub-350 BSS micron particle sizes.

04

Quality Batching

Gas volumetry, active metal assays, and bulk density verification ensure complete compliance before moisture-sealed packaging.
