Who We Are

Specialists in industrial aluminium powder.

Rohini Metals is a dedicated producer of atomised and flake aluminium powders. Founded in 1999, our manufacturing focus is centred strictly on three industrial categories: AAC block manufacturing, pyrotechnic formulations, and industrial atomised fractions.

We believe industrial trust begins with transparent physical data. Every grade we supply comes with published mesh analysis, density, and gas volumetry parameters.

Our Beginning
1999

Founded with a single core commitment.

Rohini Metals was established in 1999 in Virudhunagar, Tamil Nadu. Over the past 25 years, we have continuously refined our melting furnaces, high-velocity gas atomisation nozzles, and vibrating sieve decks to deliver uniform particle sizes and active aluminium purity.

Growth & Milestones

Our journey of continuous metallurgical progress.

From initial facility establishment in South India to nationwide supply agreements and international export preparation.

1999

Founding in Virudhunagar

Rohini Metals establishes its primary manufacturing facility in Virudhunagar, focusing directly on molten aluminium atomisation.

2006

Pyrotechnic Powder Formulation

Developed certified RMA, RMR, and RMF pyrotechnic grades with controlled sub-45 micron particle balances and low fat additives.

2014

AAC Block Sizing Breakthrough

Introduced RMDG 60 and RMC 60 grades, engineered to deliver consistent hydrogen gas aeration curves for modern lightweight building blocks.

2021

Technical Specification Standardisation

Standardised published sieve mesh analysis, active metal testing, and bulk densities across all product grades.
